      How to Use

      How FIDEX AI works

      A short guide to what each part of the application is for. Some functions are previews while FIDEX AI is built step by step.

      Assign a project

      Describe a project or give an instruction on the home screen. You do not need every detail up front: FIDEX AI starts from what you know and asks for the rest when needed. It is designed to manage work, not to chat all day.

      Take Over: circle and activity panel

      After you assign work, the full window hides and two small elements remain: a logo circle and an activity panel beside it. Click the circle to show or hide the panel. Use the open button in the panel, or the tray icon, to bring back the full interface.

      Questions and your input

      When FIDEX AI is missing something, such as a folder location, an account or the target audience, it asks in the panel. Answer there, or add information or new instructions at any time. The task keeps going while you reply.

      Approvals

      Publishing, sending messages, spending money, deleting files and external commitments always stop and wait for your approval in the panel. Nothing consequential happens without it.

      Start Solving World Problems

      A separate workspace for researching a problem and working towards solutions. It is a placeholder until the research engine is connected.

      History and Settings

      History keeps your conversations and assignments. Settings control whether the Take Over panel opens after you assign work and whether it stays on top of other windows.

      The AI brain, voice recognition and task execution are not connected yet. Everything FIDEX AI appears to do is a clearly labelled simulation.
