    How to Use

    How FIDEX SI works

    A short guide to what each part of the application is for.

    New Chat

    Press New chat at the top of the left menu to start a fresh conversation. Type your message and press Enter (Shift+Enter adds a new line). Use the microphone to speak, or the attach button to add an image. Old conversations stay in History, where you can open or delete them, or clear them all.

    Give Me Your Details

    Open Give Me Your Details and fill in only what you want FIDEX SI to know: your name, current project and occupation. Press Save. FIDEX SI uses these details to answer you personally. Press Remove my details to delete them, including FIDEX SI's copy.

    About

    Open About to see the version (Version 1), the release date and who built FIDEX: the FIDEX Team. Use the Restart FIDEX button there to restart the application safely.

    Give Me Your Details

    Tell FIDEX SI about you

    Share only what you want. Your details are saved on this device and added to FIDEX SI's memory, so answers can fit who you are and what you do.
